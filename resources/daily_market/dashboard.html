Data and indicator definitions

BTC/USD candles use completed UTC sessions from Bitstamp. Weekly bars use Monday–Sunday UTC and are marked partial until all seven days are available.

SMA40 and SMA200 use daily closes. The Z-score bands use EMA27 ± 1.7 times the 27-day population standard deviation, and the hover readout includes the price Z-score. SMA50w uses completed weeks. Weekly charts sample daily indicators and on-chain series on the last candle date of each week.

Holder metrics, stress bands and Cointime Price use BTC Floor’s Checkonchain series. CVDD identifies its provider in the checklist. Values are aligned by UTC date, with missing observations left blank.
